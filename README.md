

# INNOVATE2PROCURE

## About the Project

Innovate2Procure is a digital platform designed to simplify the journey from identifying a government problem to finding, evaluating, testing, and scaling innovative startup solutions.

The platform creates a structured pathway between government departments and startups while supporting transparent evaluation, controlled pilots, performance measurement, and informed procurement decisions.

The goal is not to replace existing procurement systems but to create a structured innovation pathway that connects innovation discovery and validation with compliant procurement.

## Innovation Procurement Workflow

```text
DEFINE
  ↓
DISCOVER
  ↓
EVALUATE
  ↓
PILOT
  ↓
PROVE
  ↓
PROCURE & SCALE
````

### Define

Departments identify operational problems and create structured problem statements.

### Discover

Suitable startups and innovative solutions can be identified based on the problem requirements.

### Evaluate

Solutions can be assessed using structured technical, operational, risk, and eligibility criteria.

### Pilot

Selected solutions can be tested through controlled pilots or sandbox environments.

### Prove

Pilot outcomes can be measured against predefined performance indicators and success criteria.

### Procure & Scale

Successful solutions can move toward compliant procurement and wider deployment.

---

## Problem We Are Solving

Government departments often face operational problems that could benefit from innovative startup solutions. However, traditional procurement processes are generally designed around established vendors and standardized products.

This can make it difficult to:

* Discover suitable startups
* Evaluate new technologies
* Conduct controlled pilots
* Manage data and intellectual property
* Define measurable pilot outcomes
* Structure milestone-based contracts
* Decide whether a successful pilot should scale
* Move validated solutions into procurement

Innovate2Procure aims to provide a structured digital pathway for these stages.
